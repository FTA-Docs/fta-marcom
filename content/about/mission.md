We’re here to give parents a fighting chance. As Big Tech and AI work ever-harder to command more of our children’s attention and time, we provide parents with the evidence-based, practical, and personalized behavior change knowledge, plans and support they need - to minimize their child’s concerning technology behaviors, reduce technology-focused conflict in the home and arrive at a shared vision of digital health.    

For parents, this work isn’t always straightforward. It requires patience, humility, a spirit of adventure and a lot of empathy - for your child and yourself. But the rewards can be significant as your child learns to take back control, engage with technology in healthier ways and reclaim more time for real life.
