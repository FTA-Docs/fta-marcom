SMS notifications and reminders are an important part of the Alignd experience but are strictly opt-in: We strongly recommend you enable them with the "Text message (SMS) alerts" toggle in your account settings after verifying your phone number. You can turn them off there at any time.

Message frequency varies (typically a few messages per week). Message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to unsubscribe or **HELP** for help at any time.
