1Parties and Purpose

This Agreement is entered into by the undersigned parent or legal guardian (“Participant”) and Family Technology Advisors, LLC (“FTA”), including its Alignd product, brand, and services (collectively, “Alignd”).

For purposes of this Agreement, “Released Parties” means FTA, Alignd, their respective current and former parents, subsidiaries, affiliates, successors, assigns, officers, directors, shareholders, employees, agents, contractors, consultants, advisors, representatives, technology providers, and other persons acting on their behalf in connection with the testing program.

Participant voluntarily agrees to participate in the Alignd Friends & Family Product Testing Program (“Program”), which evaluates an early-stage technology platform intended to help families address children’s digital health and technology-use challenges.

2Voluntary Participation and Testing Activities

Participant understands that the Program is a limited, approximately three-week product testing and research initiative that may involve:

  • Providing information about Participant’s family, child or children, technology use, habits, goals, and challenges.
  • Receiving and reviewing an initial personalized family plan.
  • Considering or implementing suggested goals, activities, or behavioral changes.
  • Completing two weekly check-ins and reviewing updated plans.
  • Completing three feedback surveys about the experience.
  • Participating in an approximately 45-minute follow-up conversation.
  • Providing candid feedback regarding Alignd’s functionality, usability, relevance, effectiveness, and trustworthiness.

Participation is entirely voluntary. Participant may decline any recommendation, skip any activity, or discontinue participation at any time.

Participant understands that a $100 Amazon gift card is offered upon completion of the three-week testing program, subject to the Program’s communicated participation requirements. The gift card does not constitute compensation for injury, a settlement of claims, or consideration for waiving nonwaivable legal rights.

Participant expressly acknowledges that:

  1. Alignd is an early-stage product undergoing evaluation and may contain defects, errors, omissions, inaccurate information, incomplete functionality, or unexpected behavior.
  2. Recommendations, plans, activities, and responses may be generated or supported by artificial intelligence, automated systems, algorithms, or other technologies that can produce incorrect, inappropriate, incomplete, misleading, or unsuitable outputs.
  3. Alignd may misunderstand information about Participant’s family or child and may generate recommendations that are not appropriate for their individual circumstances.
  4. Alignd does not guarantee that its recommendations will improve a child’s health, behavior, emotional well-being, technology habits, family relationships, or other outcomes.
  5. The Program is intended to evaluate the product and is not an established or clinically validated treatment program.

PARTICIPANT UNDERSTANDS THAT ALIGND MAY MAKE MISTAKES AND THAT RELIANCE ON ITS OUTPUTS MAY RESULT IN ADVERSE CONSEQUENCES.

4Not Medical or Mental Health Care

Participant acknowledges and agrees that Alignd is not a substitute for qualified professional medical, psychiatric, psychological, behavioral-health, or other clinical services.

Unless expressly disclosed otherwise in writing, Alignd does not establish a physician-patient, therapist-patient, or other clinical treatment relationship through participation in this Program.

Participant understands that:

  • Alignd’s recommendations are informational and educational, not individualized medical diagnoses or treatment prescriptions.
  • Recommendations should not replace advice from qualified healthcare professionals.
  • Participant should consult appropriate professionals before implementing recommendations that could materially affect a child’s physical health, mental health, emotional well-being, safety, or established treatment.
  • Participant should not discontinue or modify prescribed treatment, medication, therapy, or professional care based on Alignd’s recommendations.
  • Alignd is not an emergency response, crisis intervention, or continuous monitoring service.

In an emergency, Participant should contact emergency services or an appropriate crisis resource rather than rely on Alignd.

5Acknowledgment and Assumption of Risks

Participant understands that participation may involve known and unknown risks, including but not limited to:

  1. Psychological and emotional risks: Stress, anxiety, frustration, embarrassment, emotional distress, increased family tension, changes in self-esteem, or adverse reactions to recommendations or feedback.
  2. Behavioral and family risks: Unintended consequences of changing screen-time rules, device access, digital routines, parenting practices, household expectations, or child behavior.
  3. Physical and health-related risks: Potential physical or mental health consequences resulting from reliance on inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate recommendations, or from implementing suggested activities.
  4. Technology and AI risks: Software errors, inaccurate personalization, algorithmic bias, hallucinated information, system failures, outages, misleading outputs, or failure to recognize circumstances requiring professional attention.
  5. Privacy and information-security risks: Unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, misuse, or exposure of personal, family, child-related, or sensitive information, including information concerning health, behavior, or family circumstances.
  6. Other risks: Financial losses, disruption of family routines, inconvenience, reputational harm, or other direct or indirect adverse consequences.

Participant recognizes that not all risks can be identified or anticipated in advance.

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Participant knowingly and voluntarily assumes the inherent and reasonably foreseeable risks associated with participation in the Program, including risks arising from the ordinary negligence of the Released Parties.

Nothing in this section excuses any Released Party from liability that cannot lawfully be disclaimed.

6Parent/Guardian Responsibility and Supervision

Participant represents that they are at least 18 years old and are the parent or legal guardian of each minor child whose information or activities they include in the Program, with legal authority to provide the applicable consent.

Participant acknowledges and agrees that:

  1. Participant retains responsibility for the care, safety, supervision, and well-being of their child or children.
  2. Participant is solely responsible for determining whether to follow, modify, or disregard any Alignd recommendation, subject to the Released Parties’ nonwaivable legal obligations.
  3. Participant will exercise independent judgment before implementing recommendations and will seek qualified professional advice when appropriate.
  4. Participant will not implement recommendations they reasonably believe could endanger a child or another person.
  5. Participant will monitor their child’s responses to changes in technology use, routines, rules, and family activities.
  6. Participant will discontinue any activity that appears to cause significant distress, deterioration in well-being, or other potentially harmful effects.
  7. Participant will not rely on Alignd to detect emergencies, monitor a child’s safety, or identify all circumstances requiring professional intervention.

Participant understands that Alignd’s recommendations do not transfer parental decision-making authority or responsibility to FTA.
