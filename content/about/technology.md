Unhealthy technology use is an enormous problem. So we’ve created a technical platform that can provide personalized, evidence-based, digital health behavior change plans and guidance *affordably and at scale*. 

Our architecture places AI “frontier models” like Anthropic’s Claude and Open AI’s Chat GPT inside a much larger system in which 5 structural layers sit between the user and the raw model, with each layer adding value the frontier model can't supply on its own. 

**You can also think of it this way: The frontier model is the engine. Alignd is the vehicle, the road, the map, and (along with you) the driver.**
