At the heart of Alignd is the experience of co-founder Dr. Michael Rich. Known as the “Mediatrician”, Michael is the founder and director of the Digital Wellness Lab at Boston Children’s Hospital, a leading digital health researcher and renowned author. He's also a devoted clinician who has spent over 30 years working closely with thousands of parents and kids to improve their digital health.  

Over this time, Michael has gained deep insight into the causes and drivers of unhealthy technology use, the cycles that need to be broken and the unique combinations of strategies and tactics that can be best deployed to benefit any one child.
