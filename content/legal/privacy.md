We use information to:

  - create personalized plans
  - provide AI guidance

  - comply with legal obligations

## AI & Personalization

Your information helps personalize your digital wellness plan.

Our AI systems analyze information you provide—including surveys, progress updates, conversations, and feedback—to improve recommendations over time.

Some interactions may be reviewed by authorized members of our team for quality assurance, safety, and product improvement.

We do not use your personal information to train public AI models.

## Health Information Practices

Although Alignd is not a medical practice or psychotherapy provider, we recognize that families may share sensitive health-related information with us.

We protect that information using administrative, technical, and physical safeguards modeled on HIPAA privacy and security standards, including:

  - encryption in transit
  - encryption at rest
  - access controls
  - audit logging
  - secure infrastructure
  - workforce confidentiality requirements

Where HIPAA applies to specific relationships or information, we will comply with applicable HIPAA requirements.

## Sharing Information

We never sell your personal information.

We may share information only:

  - with trusted service providers helping us operate Alignd
  - when required by law
  - to protect safety
  - during a business acquisition or merger
  - with your permission

Service providers are contractually required to safeguard your information.

## Cookies & Analytics
