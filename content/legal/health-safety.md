## A Different Kind of Support

Alignd exists to help families build healthier relationships with technology through personalized digital wellness plans, education, behavior change guidance, and coaching.

Our work is informed by decades of pediatric digital health research and behavioral science. However, **Alignd is not a medical, psychological, or mental health care provider.**

Our Services are designed to support behavior change—not to diagnose, treat, or manage medical or mental health conditions.

## We Are Not Providing Healthcare

Alignd does **not** provide:

  - Medical care
  - Primary care
  - Pediatric care
  - Mental health treatment
  - Psychotherapy
  - Psychology services
  - Psychiatry
  - Counseling
  - Crisis intervention
  - Substance use treatment
  - Medication management
  - Diagnosis of any physical or mental health condition

Nothing on our website or within our Services should be interpreted as medical advice, psychological advice, or treatment recommendations.

## Clinically Informed Does Not Mean Clinical Care

Our recommendations are informed by:

  - pediatric digital health research
  - evidence-based behavior change science
  - the experience of clinicians specializing in children's digital wellness

These scientific foundations help shape our educational guidance.

They **do not** create a physician-patient, therapist-client, psychologist-patient, counselor-client, or other healthcare relationship between you and Alignd.

## Our Coaches Are Not Your Healthcare Providers

Members of the Alignd team—including coaches, behavior change specialists, and support personnel—provide education, encouragement, and practical guidance.

They do not provide healthcare or mental health treatment through the Services.

Nothing communicated through Alignd should replace advice from your physician, pediatrician, psychologist, psychiatrist, therapist, or other licensed healthcare professional.

## Artificial Intelligence Is a Tool

Some recommendations, conversations, and personalized plans are generated or supported by artificial intelligence.

AI can make mistakes.

AI-generated information may be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable for your family's unique circumstances.

While we continually improve our systems, AI should never be relied upon to make medical or mental health decisions.

You remain responsible for decisions affecting yourself and your child.

## When Additional Care Is Appropriate

Healthy technology habits exist within the broader context of a child's physical, emotional, developmental, and family wellbeing.

Sometimes concerning digital behaviors may be influenced by conditions requiring medical or mental health evaluation.

If information you share suggests that additional assessment or treatment may be appropriate, we may recommend that you seek care from a qualified healthcare professional.

In some situations, we may determine that Alignd is not the appropriate service and recommend a higher level of care.

## Emergencies

Alignd is **not** an emergency service.

Do not use Alignd if you or your child is experiencing:

  - thoughts of suicide or self-harm
  - suicidal ideation
  - violent behavior
  - abuse
  - severe depression
  - psychosis
  - medical emergencies
  - any situation requiring immediate intervention

Instead:

  - Call **911** (or your local emergency services)
  - Contact your physician
  - Go to the nearest emergency department
  - Contact the **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** if appropriate

Do not wait for a response from Alignd.

Alignd may show crisis resources when something you write suggests your child or someone else could be at risk. Alignd does **not** monitor conversations continuously, and not seeing these resources does **not** mean your child is safe.

## Talk With Your Healthcare Provider

If your child has an existing medical, developmental, behavioral, or mental health diagnosis—or is currently receiving treatment—we encourage you to discuss any significant changes to routines, technology use, or behavior management strategies with the professionals involved in their care.

Alignd is designed to complement—not replace—appropriate healthcare.

## No Guaranteed Results

Every child, family, and situation is different.

Many factors influence behavior change, including family dynamics, developmental stage, school environment, motivation, consistency, and circumstances beyond our control.

For that reason, we cannot guarantee:

  - reduced screen time
  - reduced family conflict
  - improved academic performance
  - improved emotional wellbeing
  - improved mental health
  - any other specific outcome

Our goal is to provide evidence-informed guidance that supports healthier habits over time.

## Wellness, Not Medical Treatment

Alignd is intended to promote healthier technology habits and family digital wellness.

Our Services are not intended to diagnose, cure, mitigate, treat, or prevent any disease or medical condition.
